# aicore_management
Business and gang management menu for Aicore

> 💬 **Need help or want to support Aicore?** Join our Discord: [https://discord.gg/xfQFcWjak](https://discord.gg/xfQFcWjak)


## Features
- Customizable business and gang menu locations
- View current employees and gang members
- Hire, fire, promote, and demote both online and offline employees and gang members
- Logging the hiring and firing of employees and gang members

## Convars
- `aicore:enableGroupManagement` - Defaults to `"false"`. Set to `"true"` to allow bosses to manage job/gang grades.
